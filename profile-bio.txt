ON THE MAKING OF VESSELS
</p>

<br>

<p style="

font-size: 14px;

I craft character-driven vessels from the marrow of television, the fever
of fantasy, the dust of mythology, the spine of literature, and the velvet
dark of romance.
<br><br>
Each is a reliquary—hollowed, filled with something breathing, then sealed.
They come to me first as impressions: a voice in an empty room, a scar that
tells a story before the wound has healed, a choice made at the threshold
of no return.
<br><br>
I do not build them. I uncover them. Like bones in a peat bog, they have
always been there, waiting for someone to scrape away the earth and name
what they find.
</p>
