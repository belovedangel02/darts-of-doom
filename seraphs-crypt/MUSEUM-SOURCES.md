## Panel 1: Of Eleven Men

**Date encountered:** 15 July, 18:21  
**Reference images:** 
- `museum-of-lost-tales-eleven-men.png` (full panel)
- `museum-of-lost-tales-eleven-men-detail.png` (detail/emblem)

### Transcription

> These were children born of humans, most of whom were born in a home near a cave or church. From the time they were young, these children showed such power and their hearts always drew them into the dark. They kept to themselves and far away from the noise of other children. Not the slightest moment would escape the eye of such children; no sound would pass their ears, not even the lowest whisper; no sound and not even the slightest changes in the sky; they had building hands and nails that had been to their palms would always thrive.
>
> Other people were in awe of such children, but they were afraid of them as well. At night other folk would come to visit them. Power is a thing that scares a person.

### Mythological Connections

This panel directly informs the **Krsnik / Shamanic** tradition in the crypt:

- **VIDAN** (vilenjak/elf-touched) — taken by the vile as a boy, bearing their mark, gifted with second sight and healing. The village fears him; {{user}} takes his hand.
- **KUDLAK** (dark shaman) — the sworn opposite of the Krsnik, leaves his body at night in animal form. Born into this power, shunned by the community.
- **ZDUHAČ** (storm-fighter) — soul leaves his body to fight hail and protect crops, present only by duty and absence.

All three embody the archetype of the "child born with power, feared by the village they save."

## Panel 2: Nemri i Hajdi

**Date encountered:** 15 July, 17:42  
**Reference image:** `museum-of-lost-tales-nemri-i-hajdi.png`

### Visual Description

Two antlered figures (stag-headed beings) surrounded by divine lightning/storm energy. Beautifully illustrated as powerful, mythological entities connected to sky and thunder domains.

### Mythology

**NEMRI** and **HAJDI** — the antlered brothers/figures from Slavic pantheon. Connected to:
- Storm and divine power (lightning imagery)
- Perun's domain (sky, thunder, raw force)
- Stag/deer-headed transformation or divine aspect
- Potentially related to seasonal cycles or brother-duality theme

### Character Potential

This is a documented mythological reference for stag/antler-based characters — far more authentic than etymology-based naming. Either figure could anchor a stag deity or antlered companion character in the crypt.
