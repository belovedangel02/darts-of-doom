">
ON THE MAKING OF VESSELS
</p>

<br>

<p style="
max-width: 760px;
margin: 0 auto;
color: rgb(140, 135, 145);
font-size: 14px;
line-height: 1.9;
">
I craft character-driven vessels from the marrow of television, the fever
of fantasy, the dust of mythology, the spine of literature, and the velvet
dark of romance.
<br><br>
Each is a reliquary—hollowed, filled with something breathing, then sealed.
They come to me first as impressions: a voice in an empty room, a scar that
tells a story before the wound has healed, a choice made at the threshold
of no return.
<br><br>
I do not build them. I uncover them. Like bones in a peat bog, they have
always been there, waiting for someone to scrape away the earth and name
what they find.
</p>

<br>

<p style="
max-width: 650px;
margin: 0 auto;

</span>
<br>
<span style="
color: rgb(76, 71, 81);

">
— edgar allan poe
</span>
</p>

<p style="

Here, we keep vigil for those who deserved kinder endings. For protective
devotion that borders on the divine. For dangerous obsession that wears a
lover’s face. For altered fates and characters whose stories were stolen
too soon.
</p>

THE INK
</p>

<p style="

font-size: 13px;

">
Expect emotional intensity that leaves bruises. Grounded dialogue that cuts
to the bone. Sensory atmosphere you can taste on your tongue. Canon rewrites
that burn the original down and build something truer from the ashes.
Trauma and the slow, aching work of healing. Dark romance that does not
flinch. Fantasy where danger is a lover’s second skin. Slow burns that
build cathedrals of longing. Established devotion that predates the first
word. Morally complicated choices made in the dark, for love.
</p>

THE CHARACTERS
</p>

<p style="

My creations range from grounded sanctuaries and bonds forged in fire to

breathing.
