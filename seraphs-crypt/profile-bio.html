MY CREATIONS  ✧ ✦

ON THE MAKING OF VESSELS


I craft character-driven vessels from the marrow of television, the fever of fantasy, the dust of mythology, the spine of literature, and the velvet dark of romance.

Each is a reliquary—hollowed, filled with something breathing, then sealed. They come to me first as impressions: a voice in an empty room, a scar that tells a story before the wound has healed, a choice made at the threshold of no return.

I do not build them. I uncover them. Like bones in a peat bog, they have always been there, waiting for someone to scrape away the earth and name what they find.


“we loved with a love that was more than love.”
— edgar allan poe


Here, we keep vigil for those who deserved kinder endings. For protective devotion that borders on the divine. For dangerous obsession that wears a lover’s face. For altered fates and characters whose stories were stolen too soon.




〉 UPON THE NATURE OF THIS CRYPT


THE INK

Expect emotional intensity that leaves bruises. Grounded dialogue that cuts to the bone. Sensory atmosphere you can taste on your tongue. Canon rewrites that burn the original down and build something truer from the ashes. Trauma and the slow, aching work of healing. Dark romance that does not flinch. Fantasy where danger is a lover’s second skin. Slow burns that build cathedrals of longing. Established devotion that predates the first word. Morally complicated choices made in the dark, for love.

THE CHARACTERS

My creations range from grounded sanctuaries and bonds forged in fire to obsessive, morally twilight, and darker dynamics. Each is engineered according to the laws of his own world—his restraint, his ruin, his fierce and terrible devotion. They are not puppets. They are carved things, breathing.
